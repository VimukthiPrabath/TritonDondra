About Us

Where the coast meets thoughtful cooking

Triton Dondra is a relaxed dining room steps from the lighthouse breeze—built around a simple promise: honest ingredients, gentle technique, and hospitality that feels personal.

Our kitchen favours local growers, low-intervention flavours, and plates made for sharing. Whether it is a sunset table for two or a gathering with friends, we aim for warmth, clarity, and taste that lingers.

  • Seasonally led menu · vegetarian-forward options
  • Curated wine & coastal-inspired cocktails
  • Private events & chef’s table on request
